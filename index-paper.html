    Chapter one: the person

    A Little About Me

    I'm a web developer with a Bachelor's in Information Technology (Honours) from Universiti Tun Hussein Onn Malaysia. I grew up in Sudan, where I ran my own small electronics business before university. That's where I learned that people remember how something feels to use.

    Today I build and optimise web applications. I find what's slowing people down, fix the technical issues behind it, and design solutions that make everyday processes simpler.

    I approach problems the same way every time. I understand the person first, sketch the simplest thing that works, then polish until nothing feels in the way. I care about building products that mean something, like HopeOasis, a platform for healthcare donations in Sudan.

    My goal is to keep growing as a full-stack developer and join a team that ships thoughtful, well-crafted products.

    “Good websites don't just work. They create experiences.”
